Practice, How to play, History, Stats and Suggest a question are all this component.

```jsx
<Disclosure title="Practice" summary="doesn't affect your streak" divider={false}>…</Disclosure>
<Disclosure title="Stats" icon="chart-column" summary="12 played · 780 pts">…</Disclosure>
```

Summaries answer the question the title raises ("how many?", "does it count?") in lowercase, no full stop. Sections stay collapsed by default; the daily question is the only thing open on arrival.
