Estimation Gym

One question a day, the same one for everyone. You are scored on how close you get in powers of ten, not on being exact. Takes about a minute.

Play now

No account. Nothing from an app store.

📱 On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Play now above.
  3. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, □↑.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add.

It has to be Safari. Chrome and Firefox on iPhone cannot add apps to the home screen — that is an Apple restriction, not a fault in the game.

🤖 On Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Play now above.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  4. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen if that is what yours says.

Chrome may offer to install it by itself, in a bar along the bottom. Taking that offer does the same thing.

💻 On a computer

  1. Just tap Play now — it works in the browser.
  2. In Chrome or Edge you can also click the install icon in the address bar to keep it in its own window.

Why bother installing?

It opens like a normal app, with no browser bars, and it works offline — the questions travel with it, so it plays on a plane or underground.

Your streak and history stay on your own device. There is no account and no sign-up, and nothing about how you play leaves your phone.

Also runs as a widget for the Omarchy desktop bar.
